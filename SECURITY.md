# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately, by email to **support@nels.money** with "Security" in the subject line. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. We will acknowledge your report, keep you updated while we investigate, and credit you when the fix ships if you would like.

## Scope

In scope: the code in this repository, and the hosted service at `app.nels.money` and its API.

Out of scope: denial-of-service and volume testing, social engineering, and findings in third-party services we integrate with (Stripe, Plaid, GoCardless, Belvo, Basiq, Akahu, Google Gemini). Report those to the vendor.

Please test only against accounts you own, and never access or change another user's data.
